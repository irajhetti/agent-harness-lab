Every tool has a marked place on a shadow board. Each engineer is issued ten numbered brass tags. When a tool is taken, the engineer hangs one of their tags in its place, so anyone can see who has it.

Personal tools are not brought into the hangars.

At the end of each shift the supervisor checks every board and signs the tool check sheet. If a tool is missing, the aircraft that engineer worked on are held on the ground until the tool is found or the aircraft have been searched.
