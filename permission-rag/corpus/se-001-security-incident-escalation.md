A suspected security breach is reported to the security duty officer on the secure line within ten minutes. It is not discussed on the open radio.

An unknown person found airside without a pass is a level 2 incident. They are kept in sight, not confronted alone, and the duty officer is called.

The duty officer gives each incident a number. Anything that may be evidence, such as a cut fence or a dropped pass, is left where it is until the duty officer arrives.
