Harrowmere Aerodrome has neighbours on three sides. Engine ground runs are not carried out between 21:00 and 07:00.

High-power engine runs take place only in the run-up bay at the northern edge of the airfield, with the aircraft facing west.

Residents can raise a noise complaint through the community line or the form on the aerodrome website. Each complaint is logged and answered within five working days. The operations manager reviews the log every month.
