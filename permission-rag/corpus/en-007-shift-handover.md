Shifts change at 06:45 and 18:45. The outgoing and incoming shifts overlap for 15 minutes.

The outgoing supervisor fills in the handover sheet. It lists the jobs still open, any panels left open, tools on issue and parts that are awaited. The two supervisors walk round each aircraft together.

The incoming supervisor signs the sheet to accept the work. A spoken handover on its own is not accepted.
