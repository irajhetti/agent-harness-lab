All visitors to Harrowmere Aerodrome report to the gatehouse on Linden Road. A visitor shows photo identification and is given a grey day pass. The pass is worn on the outside of clothing at all times.

A visitor is escorted by a member of staff whenever they are airside. One member of staff may escort up to four visitors. Children under sixteen count as visitors and need their own pass.

Passes are handed back at the gatehouse when leaving. A pass that is not returned by 18:00 is cancelled and reported to the duty manager. Photography is allowed in the public viewing area only.
