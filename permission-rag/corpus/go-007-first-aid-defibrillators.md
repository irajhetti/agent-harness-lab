There are three defibrillators on site. They are at the gatehouse, in the Hangar 2 crew room and at the fire station. Each is in a green cabinet that opens without a key.

First aid boxes are fixed to the wall beside every hangar exit. The duty first aider checks them on the first Monday of each month.

In a medical emergency, ring the internal emergency number 2222 from any site telephone. Say where you are and what has happened. Send someone to the nearest gate to meet the ambulance.
