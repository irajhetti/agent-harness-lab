Everyone on the apron wears a class 2 high-visibility jacket, fastened at the front. Visitors are lent one at the gatehouse.

Hearing protection is worn within 50 metres of a running engine or a ground power unit. Ear defenders are kept in the orange boxes at each stand.

Safety footwear with toe protection is required for all ground staff. Loose hats and scarves are not worn airside because they can be blown into an engine intake.
