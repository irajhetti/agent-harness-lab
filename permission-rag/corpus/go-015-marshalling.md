The marshaller wears an orange jacket so the flight crew can tell them apart from other ground staff, who wear yellow. Only one marshaller gives signals to an aircraft at a time.

Illuminated wands are used at night and in poor visibility. Crossed wands held above the head mean stop.

Wing walkers are used when the clearance between a wing tip and any obstacle is less than three metres. A wing walker who is unsure signals stop and the marshaller halts the aircraft.
