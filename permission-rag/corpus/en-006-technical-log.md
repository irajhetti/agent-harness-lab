Every defect is written in the aircraft technical log before the next flight. The entry says what was found, where and by whom.

A defect may be deferred only by a licensed engineer, who signs the entry and gives it a limit of 3, 10 or 30 days. The limit depends on the system affected.

Open deferred defects are reviewed at the planning meeting each Monday. A deferred defect is never extended past its limit without the written agreement of the quality manager.
