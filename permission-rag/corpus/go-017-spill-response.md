A fuel or oil spill smaller than two metres across is covered with absorbent granules from the nearest spill kit. Used granules are swept up and put in the red hazardous waste bins, never in general waste.

For a larger spill, fuelling stops and the fire station is called on 2222. Drain covers are placed over nearby drains to keep fuel out of the surface water system. No engine is started within 15 metres until the area is declared safe.

Every spill, whatever its size, is reported on a spill form the same day.
