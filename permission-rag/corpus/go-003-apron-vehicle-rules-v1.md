The speed limit on the apron is 20 miles per hour. Drivers slow down when passing a parked aircraft.

An amber beacon is switched on at night and in poor visibility. It is optional in daylight.

Vehicles under two tonnes may reverse without a banksman. Larger vehicles need a banksman when reversing near aircraft.
