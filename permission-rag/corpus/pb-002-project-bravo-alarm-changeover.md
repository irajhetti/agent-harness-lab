During the refit the Hangar 4 intruder alarm is replaced. For 48 hours the hangar has no working alarm. A static guard is posted at the hangar for the whole of that time.

The new alarm panel is in room 4.12. Codes are given out in person by the security duty officer. They are never written down or sent by message.
