Heron-E test flights take place on Tuesdays and Thursdays between 05:30 and 07:00, before the first scheduled movement.

The drone stays below 120 metres and inside the eastern box. Telemetry uses channel plan T-4. A chase vehicle follows on the perimeter road.

A flight is called off if the wind is above 18 knots or if the telemetry link drops for more than five seconds.
