Torque wrenches are calibrated every six months or after 2,500 cycles, whichever comes first. Each wrench carries a label showing the date its calibration runs out.

Before each shift the wrench is checked on the bench tester. It must read within four per cent of the set value.

A wrench that has been dropped, or that fails the bench check, is tagged and put in the quarantine cupboard. The supervisor reviews every job done with that wrench since its last good check.
