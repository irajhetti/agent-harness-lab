There are three perimeter patrol routes. Route Oak covers the north fence. Route Elm covers the fuel farm and the east gate. Route Ash covers the south boundary beside the river.

Patrols leave at irregular times, with never more than three hours between two patrols of the same route.

On Route Elm the patrol checks the seal on the padlock at gate 7 and writes its number in the patrol book.
