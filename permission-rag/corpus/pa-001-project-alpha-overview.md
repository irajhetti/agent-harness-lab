Project Alpha is a nine month trial of an electric cargo drone called the Heron-E. The airframe is kept in Hangar 3, bay 2, behind the grey screen.

Only staff who have had the Alpha briefing may go behind the screen. Mobile telephones and cameras are left in the lockers outside the bay.

Questions about the trial go to the Alpha programme office and are not discussed in the crew room.
