Project Bravo is the refit of Hangar 4. The work covers a new floor coating, new lighting and new door seals.

Hangar 4 is closed from week 14 to week 22. Aircraft that normally use it are moved to Hangar 2 for that time.

Contractors working on the refit are escorted at all times and sign in at the Bravo site office, not at the gatehouse.
