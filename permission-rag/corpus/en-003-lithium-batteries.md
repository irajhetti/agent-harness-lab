Lithium batteries are stored at between 30 and 50 per cent state of charge in the fire-resistant cabinets in Battery Room B. No shelf carries more than 20 kilograms.

Batteries are charged only on approved chargers and someone stays in the room while charging is in progress. A battery is never charged when it is colder than zero degrees Celsius.

A battery that is swollen, dented or hot is not charged or used. It is carried in the metal carrier to the sand-filled bin outside the battery room and the supervisor is told.
