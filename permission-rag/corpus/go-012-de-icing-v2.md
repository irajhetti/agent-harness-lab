Type I de-icing fluid is heated to 65 degrees Celsius at the nozzle and removes frost, snow and ice. Type IV fluid is applied cold afterwards when anti-icing protection is needed.

Fluid is sprayed from the front of the aircraft to the rear and from the wing tip towards the wing root. It is never sprayed directly into engine intakes, pitot probes, static ports or onto cabin windows.

The operator records the fluid type, the mix, the outside air temperature and the start time in the de-icing log. The start time of the final application is passed to the flight crew so they can work out the holdover time.
