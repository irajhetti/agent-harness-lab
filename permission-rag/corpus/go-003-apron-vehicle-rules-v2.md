The speed limit on the apron is 15 miles per hour. Within ten metres of any aircraft the limit is 5 miles per hour. Vehicles always give way to aircraft, including aircraft under tow.

A flashing amber beacon is switched on at all times while a vehicle is airside, day and night.

No vehicle reverses on the apron without a banksman, whatever its size. The banksman stands where the driver can see them in the mirror.

Vehicles are parked in marked bays with the handbrake on and the engine off. Keys stay in the vehicle so it can be moved in an emergency.
