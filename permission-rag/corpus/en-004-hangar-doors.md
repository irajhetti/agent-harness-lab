The Hangar 2 doors are driven by electric motors. Two people are needed to move them: an operator at the control panel and an observer who watches the door tracks and the aircraft. A warning sounder runs while the doors move.

The doors are kept closed when the wind is above 30 knots.

If the power fails, the doors can be wound by hand. Isolate the electrical supply at the red switch first. Then fit the winding handle from the cabinet beside the control panel. Keep the track rails clear of tools and debris at all times.
