Each fuel bowser is checked before its first delivery of the day. A sample is drained from the tank sump into a clear glass jar. The fuel must look clear and bright, with no water droplets or sediment. A water detector capsule is then used on the sample.

The bonding reel is tested for continuity and the two 9 kilogram dry powder extinguishers are checked for pressure and seal.

If the differential pressure across the delivery filter is above 15 psi, the bowser is withdrawn from service until the filter is changed. All checks are written in the bowser log and signed.
