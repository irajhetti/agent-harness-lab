An aircraft is towed by a team of at least three people: the tug driver, a brake rider in the cockpit and a wing walker. The tug driver is in charge of the move.

Before the move the team checks the towbar shear pin, confirms the steering lock pin is fitted and removes the chocks last. The brake rider confirms hydraulic brake pressure is in the green band.

The maximum towing speed is walking pace, about 3 miles per hour. The tug driver calls the tower on the ground frequency before crossing any taxiway. If radio contact is lost, the tow stops and the aircraft is chocked where it stands.
