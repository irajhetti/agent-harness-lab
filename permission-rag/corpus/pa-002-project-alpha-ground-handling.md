The Heron-E is never towed by a tug. It is moved on its own wheeled trolley by four people, one at each corner.

The airframe weighs 480 kilograms without its battery pack. The battery pack is taken out before the airframe is moved indoors. Lifting points are marked in green and nothing else is used to lift or push.

Wing tip covers are fitted before any move and the propellers are strapped.
