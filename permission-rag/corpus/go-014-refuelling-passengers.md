Refuelling with passengers on board, or while they are boarding, needs the approval of the duty manager. The fire station is told before fuelling starts.

Cabin crew stand at the doors, the no smoking signs are on and passengers are asked to keep seat belts unfastened. Steps or an airbridge stay in place at the main exits and the ground below the other exits is kept clear.

Fuelling stops at once if fuel vapour is noticed in the cabin or if there is a spill. It does not restart until the duty manager agrees.
