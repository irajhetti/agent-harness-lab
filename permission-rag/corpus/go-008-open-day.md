The community open day is held on the grass beside Hangar 2. Visitors park in the overflow field and walk in through the east gate.

Aircraft in the static display are roped off. Children may sit in the cockpit of the training aircraft with a member of staff present.

A tethered balloon is flown only if the wind is below ten knots. The balloon team agrees the tethering point with the operations desk on the morning.
