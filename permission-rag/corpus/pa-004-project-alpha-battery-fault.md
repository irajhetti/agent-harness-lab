During test flight 7, cell group 12 in the Heron-E battery pack overheated and reached 71 degrees Celsius. The flight was ended early and the drone landed safely.

The cause was a loose busbar bolt. Every pack was opened and rechecked, and the bolt torque was raised to 9 newton metres.

Flights were suspended for three weeks while the checks were done.
