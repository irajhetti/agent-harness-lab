Birds and other wildlife are a hazard to aircraft. Anyone who sees a flock on or near the runway tells the operations desk at once.

The grass beside the runway is kept between 15 and 20 centimetres long. Grass of this length makes it harder for birds to feed and to spot predators, so fewer of them settle.

After a bird strike, the remains are bagged and labelled. A bird strike form is sent to the operations desk within 24 hours, even if the aircraft is not damaged.
