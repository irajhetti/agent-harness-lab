When lightning is reported within five nautical miles, the operations desk sounds the horn and switches on the blue flashing lights. Work on the apron stops. Fuelling stops, headsets are unplugged from aircraft and staff go indoors.

Work restarts 15 minutes after the last strike inside the five mile ring, when the operations desk gives the all clear.

When the wind is forecast above 40 knots, aircraft doors are closed, passenger steps are removed from aircraft and light aircraft are tied down. Baggage carts and containers are braked and chained.
