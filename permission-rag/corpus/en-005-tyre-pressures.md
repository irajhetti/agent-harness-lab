Tyre pressures are checked cold, at least two hours after the aircraft has landed. A calibrated gauge is used and the reading is written in the technical log.

Aircraft tyres are inflated with nitrogen only, never with compressed air. Nitrogen is dry and does not support burning.

A wheel that is off the aircraft is inflated inside the inflation cage. A tyre found more than ten per cent below its set pressure is taken off for inspection and is not simply topped up.
