Loose objects on the apron can be drawn into an engine or burst a tyre. This is called foreign object debris, or FOD. Everyone who works on the apron shares the duty to remove it.

A FOD walk takes place at 06:30 each day before the first movement. Staff line up at arm's length and walk the full width of the apron from the east end to the west end. Anything found is placed in the yellow FOD bins.

Unusual finds such as aircraft fasteners, tools or pieces of tyre are bagged, labelled with the location and handed to the engineering office. The finder records the item in the FOD log at the operations desk.
